Player bars, party frames, a pet map, hotkeys you can change, and the overlay stepping aside for game menus.

### New

- **Player bars** and **party frames** (settings → overlay): your HP, MP and cast, and
  each party member's HP and MP.
- **Pet map** (the launcher's map button): each world and Abyss map with its zones, and a
  dot wherever a mob that gives pet souls spawns, in its genus' colour. Pick a pet to see
  its spawns and the areas it's found in, and **[+]** to track it; you show on the map
  you're on. Map pictures and zones from Questlog's interactive map.
- **Hide for game menus** (on by default): the overlay steps aside while a full-screen
  game menu is open, found by the close X (or back arrow) at the top right of the screen
  while the cursor shows. Alt's cursor never hides it. Only a small square at that corner
  is looked at, only while the cursor shows; nothing is kept or sent.
- **Keybinds** (settings → keybinds): change the hotkeys. They work while the game has the
  focus, through Windows' own hotkeys. **Alt+E** hides the widgets for now and brings them
  back (never turns the overlay off); **Ctrl+Shift+F10** arranges them.
- **Close with the game**, **arrange from the meter**, **reset settings & keybinds**, and
  small icons on every window's lights.
- Boss warning cards, the dungeon's time left, a world PvP line, and party members' gear
  on hover.

### Changed

- New look by default: Calibri Light at 14 px (your saved choice is kept; reset appearance
  for the new one).
- The pet tracker shows how many of a pet's mobs are in view; the arrow and distance are
  gone (they were too often wrong). Track pets from the pet map now.
- The launcher's bar is buttons only. The frost setting is gone: the overlay's frosted
  background is a light blur.

### Fixes

- Pet portraits always show (no more letters in their place).
- Any window of the game's own in front counts as the game, so the overlay no longer
  steps away when the game hands the focus to one of its windows.

The README is shorter now: everything the app does is in `docs/guide.md`.
