No more numbers for names.

### Fixes

- **Unnamed mobs and NPCs show as "Unknown"**, not as "Mob 2965923" or "#18366". That
  covers the few no list names (24 of the 411 types in our captures, Shugo Festival staff
  and the like) and mobs already there when the meter started. It applies on the meter,
  the target frame, the history, boss casts, death recaps and the analysis. The type is
  still in the tooltip: double-click the target on the meter to name one. Players the game
  hasn't named yet keep their #ID, so they stay apart.
- **Fights in the history are named from today's data**, so fights saved before a name
  was known get it.
