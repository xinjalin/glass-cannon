Glass Cannon moves into one overlay over the game, and opens to a launcher.

### New

- **The launcher**: the window the app opens to, laid out like a music player. It shows
  the status and the game's ping, whether the game is running, the overlay, how the fight
  is going, and your DPS and share. Its bar has the boss's HP as a progress line and the
  fight's time against the enrage, with buttons for the analysis, the overlay's
  start/stop, the history, arranging and the settings. Closing it closes the app.
- **The overlay**: one transparent layer over the game's picture holding every widget. It
  follows the game window, hides when you switch away, never takes the focus, and lets
  the mouse through except over the meter and the world timers. The widgets are the
  meter, boss warnings, boss timers, hidden cubes, the target frame, cooldowns, party
  cooldowns and world timers.
- **Boss warnings**: a dangerous cast shows big in the middle of the screen, with a chime
  and a heads-up 5 s before one is due. A cast counts as dangerous when it marks the
  ground or takes 2 s or more. You can mark any boss skill to always or never warn in the
  analysis window's [boss] tab.
- **Boss timers**: the boss's next casts from your last fight against it, as bars counting
  down.
- **Hidden cubes widget**: a compass lying on the ground points at each cube, with how far
  and how far up or down. The game only says where you are when you log in, teleport or
  change zone, so the compass points from there for a minute; it can't follow you as you
  walk.
- **Arrange**:
  - Widgets snap to a grid (8, 16, 32 or 64 px, or none).
  - A floating menu turns each widget on or off and resets the layout.
  - Press arrange again to save and finish.
- **Night theme**, now the default: near-black panels, soft grey text, a lavender accent
  and pink numbers.
- **Frost** setting: how milky the overlay's frosted glass looks.
- World timers have **favourites** and a [★ only] filter.

### Changes

- **No more meter window.** The meter lives in the overlay, always compact, so minimal
  mode and click-through are gone. Cooldowns, party cooldowns, the target frame and world
  timers are overlay widgets instead of windows. Side windows you had open come back as
  widgets.
- **Settings, history and analysis** share one look: a title bar with lights at the top
  right (minimise, keep on top, close), flat sections and small corners. They open beside
  the launcher, or on the game's screen while the overlay is over it.
- **Nothing shown twice.** While the target frame shows, the meter leaves out its own
  boss HP, enrage and cast lines, and your personal best moves to the target frame.
- Your theme stays as you set it. To get night, pick its swatch (lavender and pink) in
  the settings, or reset the appearance.

### Fixes

- The target frame no longer shows ore and other gathering nodes as targets.
- Boss warnings stay on one line.
- A window opened while the launcher is minimised no longer lands off-screen.
