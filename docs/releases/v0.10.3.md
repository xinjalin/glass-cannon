### Fixes

- **NPCs have their names.** Targeting a quest giver, merchant or guard showed "Mob" and
  a number (Elvida was "Mob 2950865"). The game only sends an NPC's type, and the name
  lists the meter had only covered monsters. 3,719 NPC names now fill in the types the
  monster lists don't name, on the target frame, the meter, the history and the
  analysis. Your own names still come first.
- **Arrange shows how big widgets get.** The target frame, cooldowns and party cooldowns
  grow with what they show, up to the height you give them. While arranging, they now
  show that full height, so a target frame placed above the meter no longer grows over
  it in a fight. Drag a widget's corner to change it.
