Shields on the HP widgets, new looks for the vitals arcs, a target frame for PvP, and the meter in Ludra rooms.

### New

- **Shields** on the player bars, the vitals arcs and the party frames: a shield (a
  barrier, such as a Cleric's) shows after the HP, in its own colour, with its amount
  after your HP (+1,215). When HP and shield together are more than the bar holds, it
  covers the bar's last part.
- **Vitals arcs looks** (settings → overlay → vitals arcs): **gradient** (dark at the
  bottom, bright where the fill ends), **framed** (a light frame like glass, the fill
  inside it) and **ticks** (thin, with notches at a quarter, half and three quarters),
  beside solid, thin and segmented.
- **Damage trail** on the HP arc: HP you lose stays as a pale strip for a moment, then
  shrinks away.
- **Arc colours**: a swatch for each arc (HP, stamina, MP, flight power, cast) and for the
  shield.
- **Target frame for PvP**: a player you target shows their class (icon and name, in its
  colour), their rank (1 the highest, 19 the lowest, as the game sends it) and their
  combat power.
- **Ludra rooms** (Sanctuary party finder): the meter's party view shows the room's two
  parties, each with how full it is, its average combat power and who's ready, and stays
  up over the room screen until the raid starts, as it does for expeditions. Everyone's
  class comes from the room's list (expedition groups too), before they fight.
- **Black market opens with the merchant**: open the Black Cloud Merchant in game and the
  black market window comes up beside it, without taking the game's keys (settings →
  general, on at first).

### Fixed

- The overlay steps aside for windows that aren't full screen, such as Attendance and
  Force Settings (their close X on their title bar).
- The meter's buttons were slow to show on hover while the cursor was out, over bright
  ground: the game-menu check took up to a minute there. It now takes a few milliseconds.
- "Packets dropped" on the meter now only shows for a minute after the last drop, with how
  many just dropped, not the total since the meter started.
