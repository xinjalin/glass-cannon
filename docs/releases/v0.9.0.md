Boss casts on the meter, hidden cube alerts, a pet soul ticker, and fights, heals and
dungeon runs that end and add up as they should, each checked against Questlog and our own
captures (a Force, Fire Temple, Deus Research Base and more). The last release before
Glass Cannon moves to one overlay over the game.

### New

- **Boss casts on the meter**, under the boss's HP (settings → boss casts): what it's
  casting with the time left, then, for skills that mark the ground, how long until they
  land; between casts, what it cast next the last time you fought it, with a countdown.
  Times run from the boss's pull, so they line up from one fight to the next. The boss
  casts window is gone.
- **[boss] tab** in the analysis window: every cast of the fight from the pull, with cast
  times and when their areas landed. Most boss skills have no name in the game data:
  double-click one to name it, and the meter uses your name from then on.
- **Hidden cube alerts**: when a cube comes into view the meter says so (gold or red), with
  how far, which way and how far up or down, for a minute after you log in, teleport or
  change zone (the game only says where you are then).
- **Pet soul ticker**: a kill that gives a pet's soul shows its progress, e.g. "Red Spark
  Ignus soul +1 · 4/25 to level 2" (5 to summon, then 25 and 75).
- **Accuracy** in the analysis window's skills: hits the target evaded, resisted or was
  immune to count as misses.
- **Heals on allies** count (a Cleric's heal on you, say), and so does HP a player's hits
  drain back.
- Personal bests are kept per character.

### Changes

- Fights end as Questlog's do: when every target is dead or out of combat; a boss that
  resets is a wipe; the party leaving combat at a boss's phase change (Kromede's Desire)
  no longer ends the fight; a quiet boss fight waits 90 s.
- A Fire Temple run is one run (the game re-sends its map as you move on), and its stages
  split at each middle boss's death, as in every dungeon. Reconnecting into a dungeon
  joins the same run.
- A dungeon counts as cleared only when the game says it was.
- Minimal mode shows whose damage counts and what the rows show (the same pills as the
  full meter), always, with the other buttons beside them under the mouse.
- Ping shows the game's latest reply.
- A player's skill on another player is never damage (heals and buffs on strangers no
  longer show as fights in "all").
- A stopped cast or a released charge starts its cooldown.
- Class skills without an icon (Dodge, spirits' attacks) show the class's icon.
- Force members are read from the Force's roster and leave when the game says.

### Fixes

- Minimal mode leaves room for the boss's HP: the fifth player stays on screen.
- Minimal mode's notices are centred, and its pills no longer sit on the first row.
- The boss cast line no longer moves the rows as it changes.
- A late hit on a boss that just died no longer starts a one-second "wipe".
- Players who joined a party one by one (not in a new roster) are counted.
- Leaving a party that refused to let you go no longer empties it.
- The Abyss map's artifact holders read right (the real message disagreed with the notes
  it was built from).
