The overlay steps aside for the world map.

### New

- **Hide on the world map** (settings, on by default): the overlay's widgets step aside
  while the world map is open, and the map gets your clicks. They come back together 0.25–1.5
  s after you close it. The game asks for the map's contents about every 1.2 s while it's
  open, and that's how the meter knows. The launcher's overlay line says "hidden for the
  world map" meanwhile.

### Fixes

- Widgets come back without flickering, after the map and after you switch back to the
  game. The frosted glass behind them now moves off screen instead of being hidden, since
  Windows flashed while rebuilding its blur.

### Not possible

- Other menus (inventory, character, skills and the rest) send the server nothing while
  they're open, so the meter can't tell when they are. Checked on a recording opening each
  in turn.
