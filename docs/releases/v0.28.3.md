Flames for the vitals arcs, and the black market window stays up while you buy.

### New

- **Vitals arcs → effect → flames**: each arc burns along its fill in its own colour, over
  any look. The outer arcs' flames go outward and the inner arcs' go inward, toward your
  character, so the two never mix and each arc keeps its colour. More flames at low HP.

### Fixed

- **Black market**: buying something no longer hides the black market window. The
  purchase pop-up dims the merchant's close X, which the overlay took as you leaving; it
  now sees the X through the dimming.
