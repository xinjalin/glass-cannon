A fight analysis dashboard, built on newly decoded combat data: crits, back and front
attacks, perfect and double strikes, multi-hits, casts, buffs and debuffs, heals and
target HP.

![Fight analysis: overview](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/xinjalin/glass-cannon/v0.4.0/docs/screenshots/analysis-overview.png)

### New: the analysis window

Right-click a player in the meter (or click the new chart icon) to open a full breakdown
of that fight, live or from history:

- **Overview**: DPS, casts, crit, back and front attacks, perfect and double strikes; DPS
  over time for the top five players, with the target's HP in a strip beneath; every
  player side by side.
- **Skills**: every skill with casts, hits, multi-hit, crit, double, perfect, back, front,
  min, max, average and time between casts. Click a column to sort.
- **Rotation**: your opening casts, and a zoomable, pannable timeline of every cast by
  skill (crits ringed, back attacks dotted, target HP shaded behind).
- **Buffs**: buffs on you and debuffs on the target over time with their uptime, and for
  each skill the share of its casts that landed with each effect up.

Filter by target when a fight had several. The newest 50 fights keep their full analysis.
Fights recorded before this version keep their summary only.

### The meter

- Heal-over-time ticks no longer count as damage.
- **Font**: pick any font installed on your PC from a searchable list (monospace fonts
  first).
- **Reset appearance** (click twice) puts every appearance setting and the window size
  back to the defaults.
- Ping is green under 200 ms, amber under 300 ms, red above.
- Fixed: the tint stepper could go below 0% and show an "invalid args" error.

### Under the hood

- The numbers were worked out from our own training-dummy captures, then checked against
  another meter's table for the same fight: casts, hits, crit, back, front, perfect,
  double, multi-hit, min and max match for every skill. The evidence is in
  [docs/protocol.md](https://github.com/xinjalin/glass-cannon/blob/v0.4.0/docs/protocol.md).
- The bundled game data comes from pinned upstream commits, so every build of a version
  ships the same data.
- The `.sha256` file now passes `sha256sum -c`.

### Not yet

Parries aren't decoded yet; the dashboard has no healing or damage-taken views; and
effects that don't come from a skill show by their ID.

Upgrading: extract over the old folder. Your settings, names and history stay.
