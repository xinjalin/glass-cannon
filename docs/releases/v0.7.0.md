Your party in the meter, and dungeon runs from the game's own dungeon messages.

### New

- **Party tracking by itself**: whose damage counts is *auto* by default: your party's
  fight while you're in a party or expedition, yours on your own. Choosing mine, party or
  all by hand still works (the button and the settings).
- **Party view** (the dmg / heal / taken button now has *party*): everyone in your party or
  group with their **combat power** and **gear score**, the strongest shown as the fullest
  bar. It shows by itself when someone joins or leaves between fights, and a line under the
  meter says who ("+ Aja joined · CP 81.5K · GS 1,635", "− Kimmy left").
- **Ready checks**: in the party finder, a ✓ marks who has readied up, the header counts
  them ("party · 3/4 ready"), and "everyone's ready" shows when the last one does.
- **History in minimal mode**: the hover bar has a history button.
- **Fights end when the game says**: on the boss's kill, when the boss resets to full HP (a
  wipe), when everyone has left combat, or on a map change, instead of only after 15 s of
  quiet (a boss that's still fighting gets 90 s). The history marks kills ✓ and wipes ✗.
- **Deaths**: a ☠ after the name of anyone who died in the fight, and a deaths tab in the
  analysis: who killed them with what, the time from full HP, damage taken (direct and over
  time), healing received, the last hits with HP after each, and the last heal.
- **Party leader** marked ★ in the party view.
- **Fight history window**: the history has its own window (the history button, or the
  settings' fight history): dungeon runs with their fights, bosses only, or PvP. Click a
  fight to show it on the meter, right-click for its analysis.
- **Cooldowns window**: your skills that aren't ready, soonest first, with time left and
  charges (from the game's own cooldowns, reductions and resets included). Settings →
  cooldowns.
- **World timers window**: Spacetime Rift, Shugo Festival, Dimensional Invasion, Watcher
  Kaira, Artifact Siege, Siege Bosses, Guardian Lord Nahma and the daily and weekly
  resets, for your region (Asia, NA, EU, LA, Korea, Taiwan), in your time. Settings →
  world timers.
- **PvP**: fights between you and players outside your party are kept as PvP sessions:
  damage dealt and taken per opponent, burst, kills and deaths, who killed you. Not yet
  checked in a real PvP fight.
- The windows you leave open open again next time.
- **Any colour**: theme and bars each have a colour picker after the presets.
- **Highlight off**: no row is filled, and every bar (yours too) shows the bar colour.
- **Dungeon runs from the game**: the meter now reads when you enter a dungeon, when its
  countdown ends and when it's completed. A run is one entry into the dungeon, however long
  the breaks, and completed runs show the game's clear time (✓ 11:33).

### Changes

- **mine** now counts only your own damage (and healing) on the targets you hit; it used
  to include everyone's damage on them. A fight's time runs from the first to the last
  counted hit, so someone else pulling before you doesn't lower your DPS. *all* still shows
  everyone.
- The analysis window stays on top of the game, like the meter.
- The settings window is wider, so every setting fits on one line.
- **Ping is the game's own.** The game's connection ends at a relay close to you, so timing
  the connection only measured the trip to the relay (10-30 ms). The meter now reads the
  game's ping replies and works the ping out the way the game does.

### Fixes

- Being kicked, the party breaking up, or a member leaving now updates the party straight
  away.
- Hits between party members (effects on allies) are no longer counted as damage.
- Damage still ticking on a corpse doesn't start a new fight.
- The buffs view shows what players put up by default ([all effects] shows mobs' effects,
  unnamed ones and passives that last the whole fight); effects without an end no longer
  show a 4294967 s duration.
- Skills without an icon in the game data (mobs' attacks) show their first letter instead
  of an empty box.
- Players' shields and mantras ticking on a player aren't counted as damage taken.
- After the party view, its rows no longer stay under the damage rows.
- The copies of party members that bosses such as Tiere and Thamon make no longer show up
  as extra players (a party of five showed as eleven): they're mobs, so their hits on you
  are damage taken.
