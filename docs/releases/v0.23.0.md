The black market window grows up: icons, grades, pet progress, and it closes when you leave the merchant.

### New

- **Icons** for everything the Black Cloud Merchant sells: skins, Theostones, wings,
  emotes, skin chests (pets keep their portraits). Gear has its icons in the game data
  too, for what's to come.
- **Grades**: each icon framed in its grade's colour, as the game colours them: Common
  grey, Rare green, Epic blue, Unique gold, Legendary orange. Theostones and most skins
  (a skin takes the grade of the gear it looks like); the few the data can't tell stay
  unframed.
- **Pets**: each with its level and the souls toward the next (Lv 2 · 25/75), named in red
  while there's more to get and in the theme's colour once maxed; **[hide maxed pets]**
  leaves those out.

### Fixed

- The black market window closes when you leave the merchant: its **Exit** back to the
  Shop (told by the Shop's second sign at the top left), or closing the Shop. It stayed
  up before, for good once you'd scrolled it.
- **Action bar**: a buff cast again while it was still on didn't reset; it ran out early.
  The game tops it up instead of applying it anew, and now that's followed (thanks Andre).
