A tidier settings window.

### Changed

- **Appearance** has four clear parts: the **theme** (with its accent, numbers, lines and
  fills), **text**, the **glass** behind the overlay (its style, colour and tint, in one
  place now) and **class colours**.
- The meter's row settings (bar colour, highlight, row style, see-through highlight, party
  order, combat power) moved to **meter → rows**, beside the meter's other settings.
- **Overlay**: how the widgets behave over the game (hide for game menus, click-through,
  fade outside combat) sits right under the widgets now, before each widget's own
  settings; the vitals arcs' long list has headings.
- **Record fights** moved to **general**.
