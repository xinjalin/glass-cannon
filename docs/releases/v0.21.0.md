Target frame settings for PvP, and no more flicker from the world map.

### New

- **Target frame settings** (settings → overlay → target frame):
  - **Class colour** for a player target: on their name, over the whole frame (to tell at
    a glance who you're up against), or off.
  - **Rank** as the game's title (Supreme Commander, Commander, Marshal, General, 5- to
    1-Star Officer, First- to Ninth-Class Soldier, or unranked) or as its number.
  - **What it shows**: each of its parts on or off (HP %, class, rank, CP, HP bar, HP, time
    to kill, cast, attacking, enrage, your best, stagger).

### Changed

- **Hide on the world map** is gone: the world map is one of the game menus that **hide for
  game menus** steps aside for (by its close X). With both on, the widgets flickered: the
  game's asking for the map's contents, which that setting went by, also comes now and
  then mid-fight.

### Fixed

- The class, text and vitals arc colour pickers kept the colour you picked only sometimes:
  each pick is saved as it's made now.
