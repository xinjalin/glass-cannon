Windows that stay readable, and a tidier meter.

### Fixed

- **The windows keep a background of their own.** Settings, history, analysis, attempts,
  checklist, black market and the map now have a solid background, as the launcher does,
  whatever the theme, tint or background chosen (those are for the overlay over the
  game). With **clear**, the history and the other panel windows used to go see-through.
  The theme still gives their accents and border.
- **The meter over a party-finder lobby** keeps its frosted background. It used to lose
  it when the other widgets stepped aside, and looked like another theme.
- **The meter's alignment**: the strip on top (time, Odyle energy, ping) and the boss,
  dummy and PvP lines now line up with the target and the rows at both ends, and each
  player's combat power sits level with their name.
