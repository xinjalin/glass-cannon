The boss casts window is easier to get to and to read, and a cast's warning can play a sound of your own.

### Added

- **Your own warning sounds** (boss casts → a cast's **sound**). Each cast's warning plays
  the chime or a sound file you pick: wav, mp3, ogg, flac or m4a, 2 MB at most. A copy is
  kept beside your settings and any cast can use it. **[▶]** plays it; **[×]** under the
  list deletes it, and the casts that used it go back to the chime. It plays where the
  cast's warning shows, with alert sound on, and at most its first 10 seconds.
- **A boss casts button on the launcher** (the warning sign). The button in the settings
  is still there.
- **[reset to defaults]** in the boss casts window: your names for boss casts and your
  warning and timer choices go, and the app's defaults decide again. It asks once more
  first, and saves your mapping to the `exports` folder before clearing anything, so
  **[import]** brings it back. Your sounds stay.

### Changed

- **Bosses fold** in the boss casts window. Each boss is one line with its name, modes
  and cast count: click it to unfold its casts (remembered), or **[unfold all]** /
  **[fold all]** for the bosses listed. A search, and **[changed]**, show what they find
  unfolded. Bosses start folded.

### Notes

- Your sounds stay on your computer: an exported mapping or setup doesn't carry them.
- Your own sounds play at one fixed level; there is no volume control for them yet.
