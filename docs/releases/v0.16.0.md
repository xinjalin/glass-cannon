A black market tracker and an action bar.

### New

- **Black market** (the shop icon on the launcher): what the Black Cloud Merchant offers
  each of your characters this hour. Open it in the game's shop (H), on each of its pages
  (Special, Pets, Skins), with the meter open, and each offer shows here: its name, price
  and discount, and whether it's bought. Pets none of your characters has yet are marked.
  Each offer also says how often you've seen it on offer, from your own characters' pages
  (each page counts once an hour; the app never goes online). **[worth a look]** shows just
  the pets you don't have and what you've rarely seen. Offers change on the hour and differ
  per character; a page from before the hour turned is dimmed until you open it again.
- **Action bar** (a new overlay widget): the skills you choose, each with its cooldown
  sweeping round its icon, the time left, its charges and a glow when it's ready again;
  then the buffs on you you choose to follow, with their time left. Square icons, round
  icons or bars. Each class has its own: choose it in the settings (overlay → action bar)
  from your class's skills and the buffs that have been on you in the last hour.

### Fixed

- **The settings window couldn't be opened again after minimising it.** It's now brought
  back.

### Changed

- The settings and other pop-out windows have no minimise button any more: with no
  taskbar button, a minimised one was only a closed one that was harder to get back. They
  keep keep-on-top and close; the launcher keeps its minimise.

### Not yet

- Skins you already own aren't marked in the black market: the game's list of your skins
  uses other numbers than the shop's, and nothing we have links the two.
- The action bar's stun and knockdown alert (the time left over your character) is still
  to come.
