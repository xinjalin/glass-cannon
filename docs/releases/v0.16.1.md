The meter now works with ExitLag and other ping reducers.

### Fixed

- **No fights with ExitLag (or GearUP, WTFast, NoPing…).** These have the game connect to
  a relay on your own PC, which passes its traffic on in a tunnel of its own, so the
  meter waited for the game to connect to a server and never saw a fight. It now follows
  the game's connection wherever it goes: to a relay on your PC, it reads it off your PC's
  own loopback traffic, where the game's messages still are; to a VPN's adapter, on that
  adapter, even when Npcap lists the adapter without its address. If the game's connection
  moves while you play (a ping reducer turned on or off), the capture starts again on the
  right adapter within a few seconds.
- Reading loopback needs Npcap's **"Support loopback traffic"**, which is ticked by
  default. If it was unticked, the launcher now says so and how to fix it: reinstall Npcap
  with it ticked.

The guide has a new section, **When the meter shows no fights**, on what the launcher's
status line means and what to check.
