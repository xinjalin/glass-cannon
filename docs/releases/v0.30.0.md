Action bars, redone: several bars, each set up beside it on the overlay.

### New

- **Up to six action bars per class**, each a widget of its own with no box, as big as
  what it shows.
- **Settings beside each bar**: arrange the overlay, then **⚙** on a bar. Changes show on
  the bar at once. Settings that do nothing for the bar as it is are greyed out.
- **What a bar tracks**: your skills' cooldowns, the buffs on you, both (a skill shows the
  buff it gave you while that runs), or the debuffs on you.
- **Your class's skills only**, under Active and Stigma. **Hide unequipped stigmas** follows
  your skill loadout, and switches with it.
- **Buffs and debuffs**: every one on you, or only those you pick (named ones only). Auras
  and other effects with no set length show as their icon, with no time.
- **Each bar's own look**: icons or timer bars, icon size and spacing, a row or a column,
  square or round, the countdown (tenths under 3 s, coloured in its last seconds), grey
  icons on cooldown, a glow when ready, the bars' width, background and colours, opacity.
- **Each bar's preferences**: show everything, or only what's running, ready or missing;
  hide out of combat.
- **Gaps and rows**: drag skills about on the bar as laid out, and put in gaps and new rows.

Your old action bar carries over: its skills to bar 1, its buffs to bar 2.
