Each widget that sounds has its own sound settings, and the overlay hides for the Attendance window on more layouts.

### Added

- **A sound on/off for each widget that sounds**, in its ⚙ panel on the overlay: the boss
  warnings, the hidden cubes and the world timers. A widget sounds with both the alert
  sound and its own on, and turning one off keeps the sounds you chose for it.
- **This bar's sounds** on/off for each action bar (its ⚙ → preferences), above its
  three sounds.

### Changed

- **A widget's ⚙ panel shows only its own sounds** now, not the whole sound section again:
  the boss warnings' panel has the boss warning and boss on you sounds, the hidden cubes'
  the cube sound, the world timers' the timer sound. The boss timers' panel has none: it
  never sounded.
- **The settings window keeps everything in one place** (settings → alerts → sound): the
  alert sound, the volume, every alert's sound and your sound files. An alert whose
  widget's sound is off is dimmed there.

### Fixed

- **The overlay still stayed up over the Attendance window** on layouts where a widget
  covers most of that window's title bar (the boss warnings' box covering 72% of it, in
  the report). The check now needs a fifth of the title bar in view, not 30%.

### Notes

- Each widget's sound switch is in its own panel on the overlay (arrange, then ⚙), not in
  the settings window.
