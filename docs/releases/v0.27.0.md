Themes with more than one colour, and every part's colour yours to pick.

### New

- **Palette themes**: **seafoam** (mint and teal over deep navy), **seafoam light** (the
  first light theme: mint glass, navy text), **scarlet** (scarlet and powder petal over
  carbon black), **teal** (ash grey and beige over ink black) and **indigo** (periwinkle
  and ghost white over space indigo). Each gives every part its own colour, and the
  windows take its background.
- **The theme's parts** (settings → appearance → colours): pick your own **background
  colour** (the overlay's glass and the windows), **accent** (headings, buttons, tabs),
  **numbers**, **lines** (borders, dividers, window frames) and **fills** (selected
  buttons, bars, filled rows). Each follows the theme until you pick one; **[all to
  theme]** puts them back.

### Fixed

- **Edge colour** couldn't be clicked with the text edge on [theme] or [none]: it now
  colours the theme's soft shadow, and picked with no edge, brings that shadow back in it.
