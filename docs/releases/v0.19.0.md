A cooldown timeline, more ways to make the overlay your own, and a meter that fits what it shows.

### New

- **Cooldown timeline** (settings → overlay → cooldowns): a bar from Ready, each skill's
  icon on it by its cooldown left, sliding to Ready, with its time on it and a flash when
  it's ready. Ready on the left or the right, sized 70–250%, by percent of each cooldown
  or by time left (marks at 1, 10, 30, 1m, 2m, 5m). Icons that overlap take turns going
  see-through, so the ones under them show.
- **The meter fits what it shows** (settings → meter → fit height, on at first): its rows,
  open skill lists and the lines over them. Off, it's the height you give it while
  arranging.
- **Party order** (settings → meter, or click the cp or gs column in the party view): the
  roster's, or by combat power or gear score, highest or lowest first.
- **Fade outside combat for any widget**: the arrange menu has a column for which widgets
  fade and which always show.
- **Action bar colours** (bars look): a ready skill's (a full bar, green at first), a running
  buff's (blue) and a skill on cooldown's (red), each any colour; and the bars' background
  can be turned off.
- **Class colours of your own** (settings → appearance), with the text on each dark or
  light to read.
- **Text styling** (settings → appearance → text): the text's colour, opacity and edge
  (shadow, outline, raised, depressed) in a colour of your own, everywhere in the app.

### Changed

- The settings' overlay tab: the widgets on top, a tab for each widget's own settings
  under them, and "over the game" at the foot. Search still finds every setting.

### Fixed

- Icons on the cooldown timeline that hadn't loaded yet stayed letters.
- The cooldowns widget is as tall as what it shows, so its list isn't cut off.
- Colour pickers in the settings closed by themselves while you were choosing.
