Every widget set up beside it on the overlay, timer pins, more from the party frames, and
setups and boss cast names you can share.

### New

- **Settings beside each widget**: while you arrange the overlay, **⚙** on a widget opens its
  settings in a panel next to it, each change showing at once. The settings window keeps
  only what's app-wide.
- **Timer pins**: pin events and field bosses (📌 in the world timers) as icons counting
  down, or a slim row each; green while on or up, highlighted when soon, pulsing when an
  alert's due. The **world boss train** adds every field boss up or spawning soon (from the
  world map: open it in game now and then).
- **Party frames**: the buffs you put on each member and their debuffs, a dead member greyed
  with ☠, the one your target is attacking outlined; grid or list, size, HP as a percent,
  value or nothing, HP warning colours, a damage trail, and sorting.
- **Share your setup** (settings → general, or the arrange menu): export your layout, theme
  and settings to a file and import someone else's. Your tracked pets, hotkeys and
  recording never go in.
- **Boss cast names to share**: the boss casts window exports and imports your cast names and
  warning choices.

Exports go to the `exports` folder beside the app.
