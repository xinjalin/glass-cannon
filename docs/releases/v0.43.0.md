Boss warnings for the Corroded Decontamination Facility: twelve cards, five of them countdowns.

### Added

- **Terminator Bargott.**
  - His **leap to the centre** (jump the red wave, drop your puddles away from the middle),
    then a countdown to the **three shared slams**, 18.2 s after the leap began: run in and
    stand in each red marker in turn.
  - The **numbered rolls**: a countdown to the first of his eight rolls, then to the blast
    that hits everyone if a bomb is left standing.
  - The **bomb phase** at half health: pick up bombs and throw them at him, eight hits.
  - The **blinding flash**: turn your back on him, then dodge the orbs.
  - The **spinning chains**: your own card when they're on you (run over the bombs, keep
    clear of other players), and one for everyone else (keep away from the chained).
  - A **puddle** under you: move out.
  - His slams always warn, though the cast is only 1.8 s.
- **Black Blood Blatt.** The **colour phase** counts down to each of its two checks (the
  colour he names first, then the other, dodging his shot before each). The **slimes** go
  step by step: his line, the white beam to step into, both again, then Fierce Toxic Aura
  Wave, with its own card (kill your slime in the last 2 s, stand in its barrier).
- **Devouring Klawfly.** Unstable Venom Sphere (take an orb only while it's purple, one at
  a time, in order) and the deadly blast (hide behind the last stone, leave as it ends).
- **28 of the raid's moves are named** where the game only says "Attack" (boss casts,
  timers, the deaths tab): Bargott's three circles, leap, rain, puddle, slams, rolls,
  bombs, hydraulic presses, flash, orbs and chains; Blatt's colour checks and slimes;
  Klawfly's blast.

### Notes

- This is from one night's recording (a first clear) read beside eight published guides. A
  card is there when the guides agree on a move and the recording has it where they say.
  None of it has been seen on the overlay in a fight yet.
- Blatt's two countdowns and Klawfly's cards rest on one fight each.
- Not covered: the Surveillance Device, Bargott's laser and black holes, and most of
  Blatt's and Klawfly's ordinary attacks.
- Bargott enrages 8 minutes after the pull; the meter already shows the game's own timer.
