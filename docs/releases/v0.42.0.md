Every fight in the history keeps its full analysis.

### Fixed

- **Older fights in the history opened to an empty analysis window.** The history lists
  the last 200 boss fights and 200 others, but only the newest 50 of each kept their full
  analysis, so one busy raid day cut the night before's pulls. Every fight in the list
  keeps its analysis now.

### Changed

- **Saved fights are compressed**, to about a ninth of the size: 200 take less room than
  50 did. Fights saved by older versions still open.
- **A fight whose analysis is already gone is marked *summary only*** in the history list,
  and the analysis window says its analysis wasn't kept, rather than "no fight to analyse
  yet".

### Notes

- Analyses that were already cut can't be brought back: those fights stay in the list as
  *summary only*, and still show on the meter.
- Versions before this one can't read the compressed files. If you go back to one, fights
  saved from now on show there without their analysis.
