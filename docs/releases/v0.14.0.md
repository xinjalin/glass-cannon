Boss mechanics in the warnings, and party cooldowns you can see in full.

### New

- **Boss mechanics** (settings → **boss mechanics**, on by default): the boss warnings now
  say what to do for dungeon bosses' mechanics the long-cast rule misses: a buff on the
  boss not to attack into (Lakshmi's reflect), a debuff on you to carry away from the
  party, an ally to free (with their name), a cue cast (Vakron's thorn rings and prison,
  Tiere's Rafflesia), a stagger check, and a pool you're standing in. 30 mechanics of 15
  bosses in 8 dungeons, written by hand; 18 of them checked against our own runs.
- **Party cooldowns by class** (settings → **party cooldowns by**): besides each player and
  their cooldowns, group them by class, then skill, then who has it (ready first).

### Fixed

- **Party cooldowns** showed only yours: the widget was cut off at the height it was
  arranged to, so the rest of the party sat below it. It's now always as tall as the whole
  party, and moves up rather than off the screen.
- Party members now count even when the meter started after the party formed (their names
  or the party's roster not seen yet), for the party cooldowns, the party scope and the
  fight's party.
- **Stagger warnings** come only during a boss's stagger check (Bakarma, Atiel) and stay
  until the gauge breaks or the check ends; not when the boss spawns, nor after a break.
- The **target frame** grows with its stagger gauge and cast instead of cutting them off,
  and shows "staggered!" when the gauge breaks.
- Widgets that fit their content grow back after shrinking.
