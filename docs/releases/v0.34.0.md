Hidden cube alerts only when you have a key, buffs that were already on you at login, and
fixes to the timer pins and the widgets' settings panels.

### Added

- **Hidden cubes: only with a key** (hidden cubes, arrange → ⚙). With it on, a cube isn't
  announced and doesn't chime while you have no Key: Hidden Cube. The settings say how many
  keys you have. Your keys are counted from your bag as you log in, so log in with Glass
  Cannon running; until your bag has been seen, cubes are announced as before.

### Fixed

- **A buff that was already on you when you logged in** (a mantra left on, a scroll or
  food still running) wasn't seen until you turned it off and on. Action bars tracking it
  now show it from the moment you log in or enter a zone.
- **Timer pins showed pinned world bosses as a greyed "up?" after logging in.** Boss times
  only come from the world map, so after a login they're old: the boss did spawn, but it
  may have been killed since. Such a pin now reads **?** after the other pins, and with
  **pins show within** set it's left off until you open that zone's map again.
- **A widget's settings panel flickered as it opened** (the hidden cubes' most of all): it
  showed at full height, then jumped to its real size. It now opens at the right size.

### Changed

- A widget's **⚙** closes its settings panel when you click it again.
