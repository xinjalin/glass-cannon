Sound has its own place in the settings, and your sound files work for any alert and for the action bars.

### Added

- **Sound settings in the settings window** (settings → alerts → sound). It was only in
  the widgets' ⚙ panels on the overlay, where it still shows too.
- **Your sounds, for anything that sounds.** **[add a sound]** takes a wav, mp3, ogg,
  flac or m4a file of 2 MB at most and keeps a copy beside your settings; **[▶]** plays
  it and **[×]** deletes it.
- **A sound for each alert**: a boss warning, the boss turning to you, a hidden cube and
  a world timer each play their chime, nothing, or one of your sounds.
- **Volume** for everything that sounds: 10% to 100%.
- **Action bar sounds** (a bar's ⚙ → preferences): when a skill is ready again, when an
  effect comes on you, and when an effect ends. Each is none, a chime or one of your
  sounds. They are set per bar.

### Changed

- **A boss cast's own sound** stays as it was; **default** now means whatever the boss
  warnings play. Your sound files moved from the boss casts window to the settings; a
  file can still be added from a cast's list.

### Fixed

- **The overlay stayed up over the Attendance window** when one of our own widgets (even
  an empty one, like boss warnings between fights) lay over part of that window's title
  bar. The check now goes by the part of the title bar it can see. Hover **hide for game
  menus** in the settings to see what its last look for a window found.

### Notes

- Deleting a sound sends whatever played it back to its chime (an action bar: to nothing).
- Your sounds stay on your computer. A setup taken in from someone else plays chimes
  where theirs named files you don't have.
- A widget lying over a window's close X itself still hides that window from the check.
