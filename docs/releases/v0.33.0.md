A cast bar that shows your charged skills, three new arc effects, and fixes from a full
review of the code.

### Added

- **Your charged skills now show on the cast bars.** Hold Hellfire, Deadshot, Punishment,
  Bolt or the like and the bar fills to the top charge level, with a notch at each level,
  and reads **max** until you let go. Until now your own casts never showed at all.
  Skills that aren't charged still have no cast bar: the game doesn't announce them.
- **Cast bar**, a new widget: your cast on a bar of its own, with the skill's icon, name
  and time. Turn it on with the other widgets and arrange it where you like. Its settings
  are beside it (arrange, then ⚙), and a sample cast runs on it while you arrange:
  - **look**: solid, thin, segmented, gradient or framed; a colour of your own; round or
    square ends; how thick; the charge level marks; whether the empty bar shows between
    casts, and whether its effect runs then too.
  - **text**: the icon and name on or off; the time left, left and whole, or none; the
    name and time over, on or under the bar; their size.
  - **effect**: the vitals arcs' effect, matched to theirs as you change it, or one
    picked for the bar with its own strength, brightness, size and speed.
  - **where**: whether your cast also shows on the player bars and the vitals arcs.
- **Three new vitals arc effects**: **frost** (shards of ice, with snowflakes drifting
  down), **feathers** (the arc plumed like a wing, a feather floating off now and then)
  and **pulse** (no particles: the arc's glow breathes and a wave of light runs up it).

### Changed

- **Player bars** share the widget's height between them, so there's no empty space under
  them; make the widget taller for taller bars. Their cast can be turned off (cast bar →
  where), and the HP and MP bars then take its room.
- **Reset settings** keeps your action bars and timer pins, as it already kept your stars
  and alerts, and the overlay updates at once.

### Fixed

- A fight with two bosses that died more than two minutes apart didn't count as a kill.
- Once the history was full, opening a saved fight could show a different one.
- Bosses weren't recognised until a restart when the game data loaded after the app
  started.
- A setup file or a hand-edited settings file with values out of range could make the app
  misbehave on start (a dummy run length could freeze it). Every setting is put back in
  range on load, and an imported layout keeps only places that make sense.
- Importing a setup with a theme didn't restyle the windows' frames.
- The meter's boss cast line slowed the overlay down over a long boss fight.
- A change in a widget's settings panel (action bars, timer pins, party frames) could be
  undone by the reply to the change before it.
- The daily and weekly reset never gave their "is starting" alert.
- With the game closed, the app slowly used more memory.
