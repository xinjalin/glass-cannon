Boss casts, dungeon stage splits and personal bests, a target frame cast bar, your Odyle
energy and tickets, and every window remembering where you put it and taking your
background.

### New

- **Boss casts window** (settings → boss casts): what the boss cast this fight, newest
  first (✕ when it was stopped), and when you fight it again, what it cast next last time
  with a countdown. Its routine skills (cast 8 or more times a fight) hide behind
  **[frequent]**.
- **Cast bar in the target frame**: what your target is casting and the time left.
- **Dungeon stage splits**: each run in the history shows how long each stage took, with a
  ★ on your fastest stages and clear of that dungeon and mode.
- **Personal bests per boss**: your best DPS and fastest kill on the meter's boss line
  during the fight (green while you're beating it), and ★ tags on the fights that set
  them.
- **Revivals**: the deaths tab shows how long until each player was back.
- **Your long cooldowns from the start** in the party cooldowns window, ready until used
  (from the game's list of your skills).
- **World timers**: your Odyle energy (basic and charged), your content tickets (double-click
  one to name it; the game doesn't), and who holds the Abyss artifacts (when you open the
  Abyss map or enter the Abyss; not tested in game yet).
- **Force DPS**: in a Force (20-player raid group), its members count like your party
  (not tested in game yet).
- **Clear the history**: [clear] at the bottom of the history window deletes every fight,
  dungeon run, PvP session and personal best (it asks first).
- **Remembered positions**: every window (the meter, settings, analysis and the side
  windows) opens where you last left it, at the same size.

### Changes

- Every window takes the background you choose (frosted, tinted or clear); the settings and
  analysis windows stay tinted enough to read.
- Custom colours are saved and shown in every window as you pick them, the meter's bars
  included. A dark colour is used as it is, with light text on it.
- The status light is bigger and has its own colour per state: green capturing, amber
  waiting for the game, red in combat (both pulsing), peach in a replay.
- The party view labels combat power and gear score (cp, gs).
- Party notices (joins, leaves, alerts) show one at a time on the meter's top border instead
  of pushing the rows down.
- A middle boss's death ends the fight even if it died out of view (the game says).

### Fixes

- Picking a colour between red and magenta no longer breaks the theme or closes the picker.
- A picked colour is no longer lost when you close the picker by clicking another window.
- With three notices showing, the fifth player is visible again.
- After a party breaks up at the end of a dungeon, its last fight still shows everyone.
- A revived player no longer counts as dead.
- The meter sizes itself to fit five rows again.
