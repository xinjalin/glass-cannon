The meter's target line, for any target, while the target frame widget is off.

### New

- **The target on the meter**: while the target frame widget is off, a live fight shows its
  target's HP, time to kill, stagger and enrage under the fight's name (and its name, when
  it's one you picked), and what it's casting under that: any target, not just bosses.
  Settings → meter → on the meter → **target hp, stagger & more** (it was "boss hp &
  enrage"). With the widget on, the meter leaves them to it, as before.

### Fixed

- The meter's target lines keep their place for the whole fight (empty between casts), so
  the meter isn't fitted to a new height each time a cast starts or ends.
- The line under the meter (world timer alerts, party joins) follows the meter's edge as
  soon as it resizes, instead of floating over the rows for a moment.
- The target frame's **attacking** and **enrage**, turned off, came back after a moment.
