### New

- **Vitals arcs: pick which show, and where.** Each arc (HP, stamina, MP, flight power) can
  be turned off and put on either side, outside or in (overlay → vitals arcs). Only HP and
  stamina, say, with HP on the right and stamina on the left. Moving an arc to a spot
  another holds swaps them, and an inner arc alone on its side moves out to the outer line
  (thanks Desperanter).
