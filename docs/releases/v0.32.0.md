Eight new effects for the vitals arcs, and hiding unequipped stigmas now keeps up with the skill window.

### Added

- **Vitals arc effects** (vitals arcs, ⚙ → effect): besides flames, **lightning**, **holy**,
  **darkness**, **blood**, **earth**, **nature**, **void** and **wind**. Each draws in its
  arc's own colour, outward from an outer arc and inward from an inner one:
  - **lightning**: jagged bolts flicker along the fill and sometimes fork.
  - **holy**: a breathing halo, with shafts of light and drifting motes.
  - **darkness**: smoky wisps curl off the arc.
  - **blood**: drops run down the arc and drip off, with a heartbeat pulse, and more of
    them at low HP.
  - **earth**: a trembling crust of stone, with chips falling and dust.
  - **nature**: vines and leaves wind along the fill.
  - **void**: dark motes are pulled in, over a pulsing rift.
  - **wind**: streaks rush up along the arc.

  With reduced motion set in Windows, every effect (flames too) shows as a still glow.

### Fixed

- **Hide unequipped stigmas** (action bars) now follows stigmas you spec or reset in the
  skill window, as well as loadout switches. A stigma you took off left the bar, and the
  bar's settings stop listing it as equipped.
