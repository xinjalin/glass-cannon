Sharing a setup carries your action bars to other classes; timer pins can wait until an event is near, and alerts chime.

### Fixed

- **Importing a setup** now brings over the action bars' looks on a character of another
  class: icon size, spacing, tenths under 3 s, hiding out of combat, what each bar tracks.
  Your own skills stay on them. Bars are each class's own: the file's classes replace
  yours, and your other classes keep theirs (an import used to replace them all).
- A widget's settings panel left open (or closed and opened again) no longer puts back
  what it showed before an import the next time you change something in it. It reads
  afresh each time it opens, and after an import.
- **World timer alerts** (the 🔔) now chime, as well as showing on the meter: before an
  event, and as it starts. Turn it off with **alert sound**.

### Added

- **Pins show within** (timer pins, ⚙): a pin stays hidden until its event is on (or its
  field boss is up) or starts within the time you pick. While arranging, those further
  off show dimmed.
- A 🔔 beside each event and field boss in the timer pins' settings, to set its alert
  there as well as in the world timers.
