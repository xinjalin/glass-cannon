Settings in tabs with search, raids and attempts, an Odyle checklist, and a round of
reliability fixes from a full code review.

### New

- **Settings in tabs, with search.** Six tabs (appearance, overlay, alerts, meter,
  keybinds, general) in one narrow column. The search over them is forgiving: letters in
  order (`alrt snd` finds alert sound), a typo (`soudn`), and common words (`opacity`
  finds tint, `fps` the update rate). **Ctrl+F** or **/** searches, **Enter** goes to the
  best match, **Ctrl+Tab** steps through the tabs.
- **Boss on you** (settings → boss on you, on by default): when the boss you're fighting
  turns to attack you, the boss warnings say so for 3 s, with a lower chime.
- **Raids in the history.** A [raids] tab for the Sanctuaries. Every wipe shows how far
  it got ("✗ wipe at 72.9%"), and the wipe that got a boss lowest is your **★ best try**.
- **Attempts** (the flag icon on the launcher, or [attempts] in the history): each
  character's tries at every boss, with kills, wipes, the fastest kill or the best wipe.
  Tabs for raids, dungeons, sealed dungeons and strongholds, and field bosses and
  Dimensional Invasions. The history's [clear] doesn't touch it.
- **Odyle checklist** (the checklist icon on the launcher): each character's Odyle energy
  and its weekly sources. The weekly Kina buy is read from the game; the two weekly
  conversions are ticked by hand. Resets Wednesday 07:00 UTC.
- **Vitals arcs**: a new widget with no box, round arcs either side of your character:
  HP with stamina inside it on the left, MP with flight power on the right, and your cast
  under your feet.
- **Arrange guides**: while arranging, a widget snaps to the screen's centre lines and to
  other widgets' edges and centres, and the line it meets lights up.
- **Map pet filters**: [all], [missing] (pets you have no copies of), [needed] and
  [tracked], and each genus on or off.
- **Boss skill names**: 928 boss and mob skills the data only called "Attack" now have
  their names (from Aion DPS Meter's data, MIT).
- **Ludra raid**: Ketu's and the Fusion Reactor's mechanics in the boss warnings, and the
  raid's mob names.

### Changed

- The **world timers** widget keeps only [★ only]; its region and "alert before" choices
  are in the settings' alerts tab.
- **Title bar buttons** are larger rounded squares, easier to hit.
- The history keeps boss fights and other fights apart (200 of each), so a run of short
  fights no longer pushes a raid's pulls out.

### Fixed

- **The meter could stay empty until you relogged.** A meter started while the game was
  already connected sometimes began reading in the middle of a message, and then ignored
  that connection for good. It now keeps looking for the next message instead. Checked by
  joining our recordings at 1,200 different points: every one recovers (in raid traffic
  the old code never did at 1 in 5 of them).
- **Fights were only saved while the overlay was showing.** With the overlay off, or
  while you were in another program, finished fights waited in memory, and closing the
  app lost them. They're now saved every second whatever the windows are doing, and the
  fight going on is saved when the app closes (or closes with the game).
- **`history.jsonl` grew for ever.** Old fights were dropped from the list but never from
  the file, and all of it was read at every start. The file is now trimmed too (as are
  the PvP log and the dungeon runs).
- **Saved files can no longer be cut short by a crash.** Attempts, personal bests, the
  checklist, pets, field bosses, content tickets and dummy runs are now written whole or
  not at all, as the settings already were. A file that can't be read is set aside as
  `<name>.bad` instead of being written over.
- **A VPN or network change mid-session**: the meter notices the game is on another
  network adapter and starts capturing there, instead of listening on the old one.
- **Other programs' traffic** (a download, a stream) is dropped as it arrives instead of
  being held for 3 s in case it was the game's, which cost memory and could push a new
  game connection's first packets out.
- Vakron shows under Vakron Sky Island (it was under field bosses), and Sealed Dungeon,
  Stronghold and Dimensional Invasion bosses under their own places.
- A relogin now clears everything kept per mob or player (an enrage timer or class icon
  could carry over to whoever got the same ID).

### Under the hood

- The target frame asked for the whole meter four times a second and kept one part of
  it; it now asks for just that. A boss's lowest HP is looked up instead of searched for.
- The hotkey watcher re-read the settings 100 times a second; now only when they change.
- `scripts/package.ps1` works under Windows PowerShell 5.1 with its output captured.
- 268 tests.
