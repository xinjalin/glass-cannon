Fixes for the black market window that opens with the merchant.

### Fixed

- The black market window that came up by itself wouldn't close.
- It goes again when you leave the merchant (unless you've clicked into it): the merchant's
  close X gone from the top-right corner, or the game's cursor hidden, tells you've left.
- It comes up on each visit to the merchant, however soon after the last; closed while you
  go through the pages, it stays closed until the next visit.
- It can't be maximized or minimized any more (maximized, it covered the game with no way
  back); one left maximized goes back to its size.
