Pet souls that match your collection, and a switch for them.

### Fixes

- **Pet soul progress** is right even when the meter starts mid-session. The game only
  sends your pets now and then (at login, entering or leaving a dungeon, some other
  moves), so a meter started in between counted souls from zero ("9/5" for a pet at level
  2). The meter now keeps each character's pets and knows them from the start; until it
  has seen them once, a soul's notice says its progress comes after your next dungeon or
  login.
- A soul that completes a level says so ("level 2!", "summoned!"): the game spends the
  souls at once.

### New

- **settings → pet souls** turns the pet soul notices off.
