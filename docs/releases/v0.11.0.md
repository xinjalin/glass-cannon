Dummy mode, a pet tracker, and when events come round next.

### New

- **Dummy mode** (on by default): hit a Training Scarecrow and the meter counts your
  damage only and times a run from your first hit (1 min by default; 30 s to 5 min in the
  history's [dummy] tab). A line under the target shows the run's time and your pace
  against your best clean run of that length. A run you keep up to the end is saved; one
  you stop early isn't. A run is clean when no other player buffed you or debuffed the
  dummy.
- **The history's [dummy] tab**: your runs with their crit and back-attack rates, each
  label's average, and any two runs compared: the DPS difference, the difference at the
  same crit rate (what's left once crit luck is taken out) and each skill's damage. Label
  runs by gear set or rotation ([label] for new ones, double-click one to label it).
- **Pet tracker widget** (settings → overlay): the pets you track, with their portrait,
  level, souls toward the next one and every area they're found in. A pet whose mobs are
  in view turns green with how many, and how far and which way for a minute after you log
  in, teleport or change zone. **[nearest]** lists every pet you still need whose mobs are
  around you, tracked or not. Pick pets in settings → pet tracker (search a pet or an
  area). Which mobs give which souls comes from Aion DPS Meter's data (MIT).
- **World timers**: hover an event for its next three starts and how long each is on.
  Arena of Tactics (10v10) is in for NA, EU and LA.

### Fixes

- Training dummies no longer count as bosses (the mob data marks them so): no BOSS tag,
  boss HP bar or "best against this boss" on the dummy.

### Under the hood

- The opcode notes (`docs/opcodes.md`) went through all 25 recordings (5.3 million
  messages): 49 opcodes are now understood, among them every other player's and mob's
  movement, targets, skill acknowledgements, loot and the login lists. Packet Lab shows
  them. `packet-lab export` writes recordings as JSON lines for analysis elsewhere.
