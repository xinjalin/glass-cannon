Combat power on the meter, new looks for the vitals arcs, and flight power in the Abyss.

### New

- **Combat power** by each player's name on the meter: your party's from its roster,
  anyone else's from when they came into view, yours from your login or zone change
  (settings → meter → combat power).
- **Odyle energy** on the strip at the meter's top, with the charged energy in brackets,
  as the game last sent it. It shows even with the time & ping bar off (settings → meter
  → odyle energy).
- **Rows as bars** (settings → appearance → rows): each row filled as far as its share,
  in place of the strip of blocks. **See-through highlight** makes the filled rows as
  see-through as the tint.
- **The party-finder lobby**: when a game menu opens there and the other widgets step
  aside, the meter stays and shows the party, so everyone's combat power and gear score
  can be checked from the lobby screen.
- **Vitals arcs settings** (settings → overlay → vitals arcs):
  - **solid**, **thin** (glowing lines) or **segmented** arcs;
  - how round they are, from straight to very round, and how wide;
  - **always show arcs**, which keeps the empty tracks up;
  - the numbers: value, percent or both; their size; an outline, a shadow, a dark plate
    or nothing behind them; at the arcs' top or bottom ends. They never cross the arcs or
    change their size.
  - the cast: on or off, as an arc or as a thin straight bar with the time left and the
    whole time at its ends.

### Fixed

- **Flight power and stamina missing in the Abyss.** The game sends them only as they
  change, so a full gauge was never sent on entering a zone and the arcs stayed hidden
  until you sprinted or took off. A value not sent now reads as full.
- **Widgets flickering with "hide for game menus"**, most likely on ultrawide screens: a
  widget placed in the game's top-right corner could be taken for a menu's close X each
  time the cursor showed. The meter now leaves its own widgets out of what it looks at.
  Not yet tested on an ultrawide screen.

### Not yet

- **Abyss points gained in a PvP session**: two of the game's counters rise with your
  kills in the Abyss, and which one is your Abyss points still needs checking against
  the game.
