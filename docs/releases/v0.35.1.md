Sharper labels for the Ludra raid's unnamed boss moves.

### Changed

- **Ludra raid: what an unnamed boss move is called** (boss warnings, boss timers, the
  target's cast). The game calls most of these moves only "Attack", so the card shows our
  own short label for what the move does. From another night in the raid, by where the
  players a move hit stood and what it brings, ten of them now say more:
  - **Eternal Ludra**: *frontal double hit*, *two rings*, *sweeping blasts*, *orbs: 4 blue
    + red*, *orbs + blast around her* and *lightning on every player* (a strike on each
    player, shared by those standing together: spread out), where they were *strike*,
    *area blast* or *raid-wide blast*.
  - **Abyssal Wings Ketu**: his weapon smash's opening strikes read *strike, side hit
    next* and its two hits *side hit*; the row of beams across the arena reads *line of
    beams*, and the hit after it *strike*.

  A name you gave a move yourself still comes first.
- The notes behind the labels are in `docs/research/boss-casts.md`: every cast seen for the
  Reactor, Ketu and Ludra, what it does and how sure that is.
