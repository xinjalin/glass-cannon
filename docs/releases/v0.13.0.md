Gathering on the map: where to find ore, gems, herbs, logs, food and Odyle.

### New

- **Gathering** (the launcher's map button → **[gathering]**): on each world and Abyss
  map, a dot wherever a material is: Orichalcum ore, gems (diamond, ruby, sapphire), herbs,
  logs, berries, vegetables, shellfish and Odyle, each kind in its own colour. The hidden
  cubes and Empyrean traces are there too, hidden at first. **✓** shows or hides a
  material, or a whole kind; pick one (or click a dot) to light it up and see the areas
  with the most. About 13,600 spots, from Questlog's interactive map.

### Changed

- The pet map is now the **map**: **[pets]** and **[gathering]** at the top of its list
  switch between pets and gathering (only one shows at a time), and it remembers the one
  you were on.
