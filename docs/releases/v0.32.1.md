The vitals arcs' effects no longer move the bars, and each effect can be tuned.

### Fixed

- **Choosing an effect no longer moves or resizes the vitals arcs.** An effect now only
  draws over and around the bars, which stay exactly where they are with any effect or
  none. Switching effects no longer makes the bars jump.
- **Width** only thickens the bars. Their outer edges and their height stay put; they used
  to shrink and move inward with an effect on.
- A widget's settings panel showed a scroll bar with nothing to scroll. Every widget's
  panel now fits its settings.

### Added

- **Effect settings** (vitals arcs, ⚙ → effect), for effects that were hard to see:
  - **strength**: how much of the effect there is.
  - **brightness**: how clearly it shows.
  - **size**: how far it reaches off the bars.
  - **speed**: how fast it moves.

  Changing one doesn't restart the effect.
- The vitals arcs' settings have a tab each for the **look**, **effect**, **numbers**,
  **arcs** and **cast**, and the effects are a compact grid.
