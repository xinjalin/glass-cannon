Hidden cubes as a plain alert.

### Changes

- **The hidden cubes widget is an alert**: one line per cube in view (gold or red,
  "nearby", or "taken" once opened) that glows when it comes into view, with a sound of
  its own once per cube (settings → alert sound, and only with the widget on).
- **No more compass.** The game never sends your own position as you move, so the
  compass only pointed from where you last arrived, and asked you to teleport to get a
  direction, which would cost you the cube.
