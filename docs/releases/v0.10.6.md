Clearer boss timers and warnings, and a launcher that stays on top.

### Fixes

- **The launcher remembers keep on top.** Its green light is saved and applied the next
  time you open the app.
- **Boss timers list only the casts worth a warning:** those that take 2.5 s or more to
  cast or mark the ground, the same casts the warnings show. They used to list every
  cast with a cast time.
- **Unnamed boss casts say whose they are.** Most boss skills have no name in any game
  data we have, so they showed as "2.0 s cast". The warnings and timers now say
  "Tiere · 2.0 s cast". Name one in the analysis window's [boss] tab (double-click it)
  and that name shows instead.
