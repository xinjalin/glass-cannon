Four requests from Discord: HP warning colours, cooldowns in the action bar's colours, a
fade of the meter's own, and widgets that hide their empty notes.

### New

- **Vitals arcs → HP warning colours**: the HP arc turns yellow at half HP and red at a
  fifth, whatever its own colour (its flames too).
- **Cooldowns → action bar's colours**: the bars of your cooldowns and the party's take the
  action bar's ready and cooling colours and its background; a ready skill's bar is full
  in the ready colour.
- **Meter fades**: the meter can fade after a time of its own (or never), apart from the
  other widgets. **[same]** keeps it with them.
- **Hide empty notes**: a widget with nothing to show but its note ("not in a party", "all
  your skills are ready", "no target"…) goes until it has something. It still shows while
  you arrange the overlay.
