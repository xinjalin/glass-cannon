Your collection, and what the black market would add to it.

### New

- **Collection** (the hanger on the launcher): every skin, outfit and wing, by kind and
  slot, with which your characters have (with the game's count, or a wing's enchant) and
  which they're missing, and how far along each slot is (greatswords 9/56). Filter by
  have or missing, or search by name. The game sends your collection when you log in, so
  log in (or change character) with the meter open once.
- **Black market**: skins, outfits and wings say **have** or **missing**, in the theme's
  colour or red; **[hide what you have]** leaves out what there's nothing more to get
  from (maxed pets too). Hover a Theostone for what it does.
- **Vitals arcs: hide when full**: an arc hides while it's full (HP with no shield on it)
  and comes back as it drops (thanks Desperanter).
