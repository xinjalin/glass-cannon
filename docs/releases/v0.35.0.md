A much lighter overlay, the game's own skill icons, the overlay stepping aside for arena
screens, and fixes to boss warnings and the group lobby.

### Added

- **Official skill icons.** Class skills on the action bars, cooldowns, party cooldowns,
  the cast bar, analysis and death recaps now show the game's own icons, a little sharper
  than before. Mob skills, consumables and Theostones keep theirs.
- **Hide for arena screens** (settings, on by default). The overlay steps aside for an
  arena match's two full-screen screens: the line-up (BLUE VS RED) as it begins and the
  result as it ends. It comes back for the fight, and once you've left the arena.
- **A shield in the vitals arcs' effect.** A shield on you has the chosen effect too, in
  the shield's colour, along its stretch of the HP arc.

### Fixed

- **The overlay's widgets slowed down in long fights**, most on a fast screen (120 Hz and
  up): the overlay was drawn again whole for every frame of the screen that anything in it
  moved. Widgets now update together, leave alone what hasn't changed, and boss warnings
  and timers are written twenty times a second. In a raid on a 120 Hz screen the overlay
  went from more than a full processor core to under a fifth of one.
- **A mechanic's warning stayed up after a wipe** (Ludra's Trial, for up to 45 seconds):
  the cast that ends it never comes once everyone's dead. A kill or a wipe now ends it.
- **The group lobby showed six in a party of five** after the leader moved someone to
  another slot, and a room's ready marks needed both parties filled.

### Changed

- HP, MP and cast readings on bars and arcs move to a new value in a few quick steps
  rather than a slide.
- An action bar's **ready glow** is a still glow for a moment, and the party frame the
  boss is attacking pulses with a glow inside its rim.
