The widgets get out of the way of reward screens, and can let every click through.

### New

- **Reward screens hide the widgets.** With **hide for game menus** on, the overlay now
  also steps aside for reward screens (a Monster Cube's after content, Shugo's…), before
  and after the cards are turned. These have no close X at the top right, so the meter
  looks for their gold button at the bottom middle instead (**Selection Complete**, or
  **Confirm**). As with the corner, the strip is only looked at while the cursor shows,
  never saved or sent.
- **Click-through** (settings → overlay, off by default): every click goes through the
  widgets to the game, the meter's, the world timers' and the pet tracker's too. The
  other widgets only show things and always let clicks through. Arranging the widgets
  still takes the mouse, so they can be moved and resized.
