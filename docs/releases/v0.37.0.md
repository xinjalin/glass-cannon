The deaths tab shows what led to each death, and what the deaths say about the fight.

### Added

- **A death's timeline** (analysis → deaths; click a death). From the last time they were
  at full HP (15 s at least, a minute at most) to the death: their HP with any shield over
  it, each hit and heal, the boss's casts under it (red: the one that killed; amber: ones
  the overlay warns of) and what mobs had put on them, a bleed or a knock-down. Hover it
  for the moment under the pointer.
- **The killing blow**: from how much HP and how far over, the cast it came from, and
  whether the overlay warns of that cast (mark it in the [boss] tab if it doesn't).
- **The same move on everyone else**: how many other players it hit within 2 s and how
  many it reached for nothing, so you can tell a move people dodge from one nobody can.
- **Where they stood** from the boss (distance; in front of it, beside or behind), the
  boss's HP then, the mechanic going on, who died with them, and how many healers were
  alive.
- **The fight at a glance**: a strip across the top with every death over the boss's HP
  (click one), and **what killed**, by move, for this fight and over the session's fights
  against the same boss.

### Changed

- **A hit taken from a mob is called as its cast is** in the deaths tab: our label for the
  move, or for the cast it's part of, where the game only says "Attack". A move with no
  label still shows its number.

### Notes

- Fights saved before this version show the list and the hits, not the timeline: what it
  needs wasn't kept.
- Your own position is only known at the moments you're hit, and the boss's facing only
  when it cast in the last 15 s, so "where they stood" is sometimes missing.
