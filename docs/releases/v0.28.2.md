### Fixed

- **Skills tab**: a skill no longer shows on several rows. The extra hit a specialty
  triggers (Judgment, Punishing Strike, Ruinous Blow, Deadshot, Drill Dart, Blaze and
  more) comes with its own skill ID and cast; it now counts in its skill's damage and
  hits, not as a cast or a row of its own. The specialties column shows the ones the
  player's casts show (Judgment **3 · 4 · 5**, not 3 · 4).
- The same in the meter's skill rows, the rotation (one lane and one mark per cast), buffs
  per skill, healing, and fights saved before this version.
- Parts the game names apart (Smashing Blow, Crimson Flames, the spirits' attacks) keep
  their own row.
