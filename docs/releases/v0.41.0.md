The boss warnings count down through Eternal Ludra's scripted block, step by step.

### Added

- **A countdown through Eternal Ludra's phase 1 block.** When she starts closing the arena
  in, the boss warning card counts down to each thing that follows, saying what it is and
  what to do: the arena closing, the orbs and tornado lanes, the breath, the lightning on
  everyone, the orbs and lanes again, the breath again, and the red circle. The block is
  the same every time (37 s), so the times are exact to a few tenths.
- **The breath step knows who has the eye.** With the eye mark on you, your card says the
  breath is coming at you and to keep it off the raid; everyone else is told to get out of
  the cone.

### Changed

- **Three of Ludra's casts are named better** (boss casts, timers, the deaths tab): "arena
  closes in" (it had no name), "orbs + tornado lanes" (was "orbs + blast around her") and
  "red circle" (was "raid-wide pulses").

### Notes

- Each step of the block chimes as it comes up: seven chimes over the 37 s.
- For the red circle the card sends you out of the centre onto the outer floor, where
  there are only small ticks to heal through.
- If the eye's holder dies before the breath, it goes at a random player; the card can't
  tell, and still says "at the eye player".
- The countdown stops if Ludra's Trial starts.
